Conclusions

Summary Trendyol

TRENDYOL.COM is chosen because it has plenty of different fashion items and fast deliveries. It is also preferred by almost half of the participants (40%) since it is locally dominant and provides good customer services

↗ Visit Trendyol.com

Summary Temu

TEMU.COM is preferred due to low prices and numerous products that are not always available somewhere else. It is preferred by 25% of the surveyed because of its variety of products and low prices

↗ Visit Temu.com

Summary Amazon

AMAZON.COM is chosen due to its trustworthy nature and good customer experience. It is chosen by 20% of respondents because of its availability internationally and trustworthy nature

↗ Visit Amazon.com

Summary Hepsiburada

HEPSIBURADA.COM is chosen as a trustworthy competitor that has good infrastructure and is available locally. It is chosen by 10% of our survey participants

↗ Visit Hepsiburada.com

Why do You Choose E-commerce?

E-commerce websites provide the opportunity to shop anytime from anywhere and compare prices immediately. Almost all (85%) of our group is used to using e-commerce regularly

Barriers to Shop Online

Security issues and need to see products before purchase make some people shop offline. It is 15% of the total number of our respondents
